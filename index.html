🥊 Why The UFC Should Add Secondary Audio Channels

1. Corner Tactics

Listen to real-time coaching instructions during critical championship rounds without commentary overlap.

2. Pure Octagon Sound

Experience raw canvas strikes, heavy leg kicks, and natural crowd roars just like sitting cageside.

3. Subscriber Choice

NFL, FIFA, and F1 streams offer audio toggles. UFC Fight Pass and PPV subscribers deserve audio choice!

4. Win-Win Solution

Casual fans keep traditional audio, while hardcore fans get custom commentary channels.
